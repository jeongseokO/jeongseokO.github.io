I am a Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Data Science, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Jay-Yoon Lee in the SKI-ML Lab. I received my M.S. from the same school and my B.S. in Industrial Engineering from Hanyang University.

My research asks how large language models can be made cheaper to run and more reliable in what they generate. My recent work, [Latent Self-Consistency](/research/lsc/) (AAAI 2026), picks the most consistent of several sampled responses using learned summary-token embeddings. A single selection method then works for both short and long answers, while adding at most 0.9% to inference time.
