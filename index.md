I'm a Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Data Science, Seoul National University, where I'm advised by Prof. Jay-Yoon Lee in the SKI-ML Lab. Before that, I did my M.S. at the same school and my B.S. in Industrial Engineering at Hanyang University.

I work on large language models. Most of what I do comes back to two questions: how to make these models cheaper to run, and how to tell when their answers can be trusted. My latest paper, [Latent Self-Consistency](/research/lsc/) (AAAI 2026), is about the second.
